Welcome to Tidewater

We are a family of coffee lovers who started roasting in a garage near the harbor in 2014 and since then we have been obsessed with finding the best green coffee from small producers around the world, which we roast in small batches on a vintage drum roaster so that every bag you receive tastes exactly the way the farmers intended it to taste.
